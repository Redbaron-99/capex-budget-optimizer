Most capex plans are built to spend the budget. That is exactly why they overspend.

I built an optimization model to decide which of 40 capital projects to fund over 3 years: $4M, 6 branches, safety fixes, yard systems, EV equipment and a new parts depot.

The math was the easy part. The lessons were not what I expected:

📌 Planning to 100% of budget overspent in over 99% of 5,000 simulated futures. A 10% reserve cut that to 13%.

📌 The highest-ROI plan was the wrong plan. It skipped a mandatory safety fix and funded two projects that solve the same problem.

📌 Every guideline has a price. Some spend targets cost nothing. Letting unspent budget carry over added return.

📌 Across 300 scenarios, 17 projects were clear yeses and 9 were coin flips. That is where the analysis time should go.

📌 The biggest coin flip, depot vs shuttle, is a network question. A budget model can't answer it. That is my next step, in anyLogistix.
